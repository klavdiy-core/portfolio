Broke: minus 26 mm

The check was green while the tire stuck out past the wheel arch. It compared two maximums taken from different places on the suspension assembly.

Who caught it. A check we run on our own work. The acceptance check and the builder both missed it.

Now: 1.5 mm

We measure the gap on a grid of angles above the wheel, comparing each point of the arch edge with the widest part of the tire. Under 1 mm, the build fails.

Not measured. A steered wheel.
